# Intent — read the whole board pack like a leadership brief

The pack has become an evidence page. Successive honesty passes each appended a qualification to the sentence in
front of them, and the result is correct but hard to read: a 260-word opening assessment, 200- to 300-word
paragraphs, and skim surfaces carrying job counts, entry-group mechanics and fixture bookkeeping that belong one
click away.

A VP reading this before a meeting should get, in order: what happened, what we can do today, what is still open,
and what we are asking for. The evidence must still be there, and every limit must still sit beside the claim it
limits — but as prose a person reads, not an audit trail they parse.

This pass changes voice and shape only. Not one claim, limit, number or link is dropped, softened or moved further
from the claim it qualifies. If a sentence is hard to read *because the truth is complicated*, it stays complicated;
what goes is the memo cadence, the repeated inventory, and the paragraph that carries five ideas.

The narrower 2026-09-06 tone pass covered the design section
([`intent-design-human-tone.md`](intent-design-human-tone.md)). This one covers the whole reader-facing pack.
