# Intent — board-pack honesty after Pass 2 measured the ordinary-SQL retrieval cells

The merged Pass 2 of the 2026-09-19 Slice B work turned a sentence the board pack repeats into a false one. Several
surfaces still say the ordinary-SQL comparison is **a declared plan with no numbers in it yet**, that it **remains
predeclared and empty**, and that **none of the nine planned cells is complete** in a way that reads as zero retrieval
fill. On 9 September 2026 one on-demand campaign filled all four retrieval cells of that comparison: forced-seed and
natural-question shapes, one request at a time and five at once, a hundred measured attempts each with every attempt
retained. The card now reads `RETRIEVAL_MEASURED`.

Correct those sentences and nothing else. The narrow new fact is *retrieval time on ordinary SQL*, on on-demand
capacity, on Acme's invented corpus. It is not the full path from question to released number, whose two cells still
have no runner and no selected version of the fact data. It is not cost: the five cost cells are still unmeasured.
It is not the graph benchmark, whose nine cells are still 0 of 9. And it is not an accepted threshold: every threshold
on the evaluation card stays proposed, five-at-once stays a measured setting rather than an agreed one, and the
slowdown at five at once has no established cause. A measurement against a proposed threshold is not an acceptance of
it, and the page must not say the comparison is finished, or that either engine won.

Where the page states the proposed latency threshold it now also states what ordinary SQL measured, because a reader
weighing a five-second proposal deserves the numbers that exist. The measured values are given as retrieval times at a
stated shape, concurrency and capacity; no sentence turns them into a verdict.

The combined bar, the access story and the Knowledge Publications product callout are untouched. Keep the human tone:
no campaign identifiers, verdict labels, pull-request numbers, commit hashes or personal names in reader-facing prose;
those belong here and in the spec.
