# Customer revenue (legacy)

The pre-2026 revenue metric: **gross** invoice amounts, attributed by
**shipping country**, with no activity requirement and no eligibility filter.

Superseded by [active-customer revenue](active-customer-revenue.md), affirmed
2026-06-20. Kept for interpreting reports produced before Q1 2026. Do not use
for new questions: for Germany specifically, shipping-country attribution
inflates the number with fulfillment routed through Frankfurt for other EU
billing countries.
