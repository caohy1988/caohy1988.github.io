Academy

1. LoCoMo-Conv — distinguish retrieved evidence from useful memory use

When Users Don’t Ask, arXiv:2609.03467 · September 3 · full PDF, 21 pages, §§4–7, Table 4, §7.3 and limitations. Reconsidered after the September 3 20:34 abstract-level exclusion.

The benchmark holds conversation memories fixed and varies query framing. Across five systems, it scores evidence retrieval separately from generated responses. AnchorMem’s recall@10 is 0.659 for direct conversational questions versus 0.368 for implicit requests. On a selected 332 cases where oracle memory still earns zero explicit-fact credit, oracle responses receive a 55.1-point faithfulness preference margin over no-memory responses. That is a judge win-rate difference on a conditioned subset, not an overall accuracy gain.

The full text supplies the reason to reconsider: absent explicit fact recall can coexist with useful contextual support. Limits include ten underlying conversations, generated rewrites, one main answer model and model-based judgments; human rewrite validation covers 40 items per style. These results do not establish production memory-system rankings.

BQAA TL;DR: pair explicit and implicit requests over the same evidence. Preserve retrieved-record references and distinguish retrieval recall, fact use and response support. Add no-memory and irrelevant-memory controls before attributing usefulness to retrieval.

2. Spurious CoT termination — count the generation after the injected boundary

“</think> Doesn’t Stop Reasoning,” arXiv:2609.03633 · September 3 · full PDF, 35 pages, §§3–6, Figure 2 and limitations.

Across four open reasoning models, five benchmarks and two early-exit methods, an injected end-of-think token can be followed by reasoning-like text and another end-of-think token. In the R1-Distill-14B/DEER/MATH slice, regeneration is absent in 51 natural-exit cases and occurs in 21.7% of 23 budget-limit cases; early-exit results vary with compression. Those are conditional counts, not a general failure rate.

Attention intervention reduces the behavior, but the authors explicitly cannot verify the model’s internal cognitive state. Their evidence concerns observable generation under injected-token controls. It does not establish a provider billing error or failure of an API-enforced output limit.

BQAA TL;DR: record the requested budget, how the exit occurred and complete token usage. Compare total generation and task quality when claiming savings. Treat delimiter-derived phases as parser labels. This adds an efficiency-measurement case beyond the already-covered tool-call censoring paper, without reopening that card.
