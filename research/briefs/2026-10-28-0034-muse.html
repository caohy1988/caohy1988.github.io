1. Continue or Restart? The Limits of Early Failure Prediction in Coding Agents — https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35971

Full PDF read (all sections, Tables I–VI, threats §VI, references). Srinath Srinivasan, Lunxiao Li, Tim Menzies, Wesley Assuncão (NC State), arXiv:2610.35971v1 [cs.SE] 26 Oct 2026.

The paper mines 147,002 execution traces from two public corpora across four model–framework settings (Table I: OpenHands/Qwen3-Coder 67,074 runs; SWE-agent/Qwen3.5 15,098; OpenHands/Qwen3.5 30,957; OpenHands/MiniMax-M2.5 33,873; outcome ICC ≥ 0.71 in every setting) and asks whether an early trajectory prefix can tell a doomed attempt from a viable one on the same task. It cannot, at decision-relevant horizons: at 20k transcript tokens — by which point 87.51% of primary-corpus runs have already run a test and 32.80% have edited code — within-task AUC is 0.49–0.50 for action-count models, task-conditional rankers (0.50 [0.49, 0.51] over 38,447 pairs), cross-setting transfers, and a three-seed LoRA Qwen3-0.6B neural monitor over 32,406 attempts (AUCwithin 0.50 [0.48, 0.51], Table II), in every solvability tier (Table III). Pooled AUC looks healthy only because it ranks task difficulty: a task-rate oracle reaches pooled 0.97 while staying at 0.50 within task by construction (§IV-C1, Fig. 2).

The policy replay is the sharper result. Under a shared 75k-token cap, learned restarting beats matched random restarting by up to +1.98 points in two settings and loses in two (Table IV) — and an exact algebraic decomposition (Eqs. 4–6, Table V) shows the entire sign is set by between-task stopping allocation (−0.70 to +1.75 points) while attempt-level selection contributes −0.11 to +0.24 points. Against uninterrupted continuation, cold restarts are Pareto-dominated in 216 of 224 budget–threshold configurations, and in all 56 at the 75k cap (Table VI): continuation solves more at lower mean token cost. The authors' evaluation standard (§V-d) — report within-task AUC with permutation controls, and compare stopping rules against both matched-random and continuation under a shared budget — is directly adoptable for any run-level intervention BQAA measures. To its credit, the paper names its own concurrent neighbours (a task-difficulty disentangling study and a math-reasoning analogue, §II-d) and scopes its claim: linear/embedding classifiers plus one 0.6B monitor, open-weight settings, terminal-outcome supervision only (§VI). This extends, at far larger scale and with the replay/decomposition machinery, the difficulty-confound line already in the catalog (difficulty-confound-early-warning-within-task-auroc, arxiv-2609-02783-earlyeval-early-outcome-prediction, arxiv-2610-25525-stoploss-outcome-trained-monitor-cost-per-retained-solve).

2. Context Is a Pipeline, Not a Window: Information Attrition in LLM Agent Harnesses — https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36114

Full PDF read (all sections, Tables I–II, threats §VI, references). Yuvraj Sehgal, Mahsa Panahandeh, Naser Ezzati-Jivan (Brock / UOttawa), arXiv:2610.36114v1 [cs.SE] 26 Oct 2026.

A measurement paper about the gap between what a tool produced and what the model (and therefore the trace) ever saw. In a kernel-trace root-cause agent, the largest prompt across 720 runs used 5.75% of a 400k window, yet 3,827 of 11,189 tool results (34%) were cut before delivery and every cut produced broken JSON (§III-A). Because the harness prefix-slices serialized JSON at a fixed length, loss is positional, not random: one field reached the model in 4.7% of 742 calls while its neighbour in the same payload arrived 99.3% of the time, and diagnosis accuracy split along exactly that line (spawned-process faults 40–55/60 vs already-running-container faults 0–24/60, §I). Replaying 864 recorded results through both cutters is deterministic — every over-budget result breaks under the slice, none under a row-aware cut — and on one production-scale trace the shipped cut delivers 0 of 128 late-list records, deleting the culprit's entire evidence class (§III-B, Fig. 1). A source survey of seven frameworks finds two general-purpose position-based cutters (a 500-char prefix slice in langchain-core 1.6.12; middle-deletion over 20k chars in smolagents 1.26.0), three that never cut, and one structure-aware line cutter (§III-C) — worth verifying against current versions before quoting externally, but the pattern is the point.

The registered controlled experiment (§IV, Table II) then refutes the authors' own accuracy prediction in an instructive way: with the culprit recoverable by a second route, the defect costs work, not answers — tool calls 53→68→76 (+43%), computations roughly double, yield (findings ÷ computations) falls 0.61→0.31, accuracy unchanged. Cost-first, errors-later is precisely the failure signature that outcome-only dashboards miss. The paper's five-boundary pipeline model (produce→deliver→act→retain→transfer→persist, Table I) with a per-boundary loss count and a yield number is a concrete instrumentation spec: for BQAA it argues for first-class "produced vs delivered" fields in trace schemas, because, as the authors put it, a run log records what a tool delivered, not what it produced — a loss on the way into the log cannot be found in the log. Limits are stated plainly (§VI): one harness, one domain, one model; the field accuracy split remains correlation; 5 recordings per fault class with paired repeats.

3. Monitor Accuracy Isn't the Answer: A Decision-Value Framework for Evaluating AI Agent Restart Monitors — https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36544

Full PDF read (all sections, Tables 1–10, limitations §7.3, appendices A–G). Siddharth Jain, Venkat N Vedam (Intuit), arXiv:2610.36544v1 [cs.AI] 27 Oct 2026.

The companion piece to 35971, from the decision side. Its alarm-to-action identity (Proposition 4.1, §4.1) shows that for an independent clean restart, success change is G = (1−q)τp − qϕ(1−p): with continuation success q = 0.5 and a fixed 90%-accurate alarm (τ = 0.9, ϕ = 0.1), the same detector swings from a 5-point loss (p = 0) to a 35-point gain (p = 0.8) purely on p, the replacement attempt's residual-budget success probability — "restartability" — which no accuracy or AUROC number reports. A second result (Proposition 4.3, §4.2) gives context compression a decision test: value is lost exactly when a compressed observation mixes positive and negative continuation advantages, so trace fidelity and token retention are the wrong metrics for judging a progress signal. The third piece is an auditable procedure: simultaneous Clopper–Pearson intervals over existing traces that certify "restart" or "continue" per task–time cell, or return an explicit abstain (§4.3, Proposition 4.4).

Applied to 194 externally verified mini-swe-agent executions on 19 SymPy issues (Tables 2–6), the framework dismantles its own headline: the +2.29-point restart gain on one model traces to a single surviving failed attempt on one task (§6.3, Table 7); the same cutoff costs −42.29 points on a second model (Table 4); the gain does not replicate on two prospective cohorts (pooled −0.21 [−4.21, 2.53], Table 6); and 0 of 103 live task–time comparisons meet the certification bar (§6.3, Fig. 4). Two side results are independently useful for harness work: fresh-context recovery eliminated the format-error terminal label in 90% of treated attempts while leaving success at 0/20 and raising cost 56.7% (§6.5, Table 9) — failure-mode movement is not task progress — and neither clean patch-unions (0/19) nor a failed-branch resolver (0/4) produced a verified fix (§6.6, Table 10). The authors are explicit that this is an evaluation standard, not a deployed policy: the audit is retrospective, five attempts per task is thin, and no learned monitor is tested head-to-head (§5.8, §7.3). For BQAA, the adoptable output is the reporting trio — alarm discrimination, residual-budget restartability, and statistical support (with abstention) — as the minimum evidence bar before any monitor-triggered restart ships.

Six-week EV

  • Within-task evaluation becomes the default ask (2–6 weeks). Two independent papers in one batch (147k-trace mining + decision-value audit) plus the catalog's existing difficulty-confound and StopLoss cards now form a consistent standard: pooled monitor metrics are not evidence. Expect monitor vendors and internal proposals to be asked for within-task discrimination, matched-random and continuation baselines, and restartability numbers. BQAA can answer that ask natively if attempt grouping by task is a first-class query pattern.
  • "Produced vs delivered" telemetry (1–4 weeks to prototype). The attrition paper gives a cheap, deterministic check any harness can run without a model: replay recorded tool results through the delivery cutter and count breakage. If two surveyed frameworks cut positionally by default, ADK/BQAA-adjacent stacks likely have at least one such cutter; finding it is a one-afternoon audit with a clear before/after yield metric.
  • Restart/early-stop features face a higher evidence bar (ongoing). Continuation dominating cold restarts in 216/224 configurations, and 0/103 certified restart decisions, together suggest near-term product value sits in state-preserving recovery and pre-execution routing/budgeting (the 35971 paper's scaffold effect, 14.06 points, dwarfs its 1.98-point restart effect) rather than in failure alarms. Roadmaps that lead with "kill bad runs early" will need attempt-level evidence to survive review.
  • Cost-before-errors as an alerting signal (2–6 weeks). Attrition and context-rejuvenation both show damage appearing in calls/tokens/dollars while success labels stay flat. Dashboards that alert on yield (findings or verified steps per computation/token) catch this class weeks earlier than outcome alerts.

TL;DRs for BQAA

  • 2610.35971: At 147k traces, early prefixes cannot rank attempts on the same task (AUC ≈ 0.50) and cold restarts lose to continuation in 216/224 configs — require within-task metrics and continuation baselines for any early-stop claim.

  • 2610.36544: Judge restart monitors by decision value — alarm quality × residual-budget restartability × statistical support, with an explicit abstain — never by accuracy alone.
