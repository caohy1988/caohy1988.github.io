3. Schema / EV notes that strengthened

  • Eval row: termination_reason (complete, budget, timeout, error) beside score and aggregation metadata.
  • Feedback row: source_role plus exactly one typed anchor (session, trace, or span) and its id.
  • Eval instrument: evaluator_kind, rubric/question id, judge snapshot and decoding configuration, aggregation definition, dataset version, and composite membership.
  • Run/session: agent or pipeline version, replicate_group_id, draw_index, and stable decoding configuration.
  • Decision/tool event: stable action_type; refusal reason_type; raw error payload returned to the agent; explicit gate/validator event type.
  • Derived-event lineage: nullable repeated source_event_ids on votes, recommendations, and approvals; commitment/promotion events cite the justifying eval event.
  • Normalization: raw source attributes retained beside normalized columns, with producer scope and normalizer identity/version.
  • Step-level eval citation: cited step id plus outcome_stratum (success or failure).
