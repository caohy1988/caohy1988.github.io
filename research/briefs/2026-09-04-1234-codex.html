Industry

1. Gemini Enterprise Projects makes one shared project resolve to a different effective knowledge boundary for each member (September 4, GA)

The dated September 4 Gemini Enterprise release note says users can create projects for personal work or team collaboration, upload files, and chat with an assistant grounded in project files and web search; the feature is GA behind an administrator-controlled toggle. The linked Projects documentation, also updated September 4, says the Knowledge tab defines the conversation context boundary and excludes other enterprise data sources unless they are explicitly added. A knowledge base can include local files, Drive files, notes, and connector-backed documents; Google documents a limit of 50 files and linked documents. Linked Drive and connector documents keep their source-system permissions, members cannot even see names they lack permission to access, and the assistant uses only documents accessible to the member asking the question; locally uploaded files instead become visible and downloadable to every project member. Linked content and original sharing permissions synchronize in real time, making the effective retrieval corpus both principal-dependent and time-dependent. A project may contain multiple chats, but those chats are private to their individual users even when the project is shared. Google does not document a trace field, corpus snapshot, or replay contract here, so none is inferred.

TL;DR for BQAA. Treat project or workspace identity as context, not sufficient provenance: preserve the acting principal's opaque identity, the sources actually retrieved, and their resolvable versions or timestamps when available. For ADK interoperability tests, run the same prompt as two members with different document access and verify that analytics can explain the different effective context without exposing inaccessible document names.
