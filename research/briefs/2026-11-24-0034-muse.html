One industry release clears the product bar: LangWatch v3.28.0 puts agent-to-agent traffic behind a proxy with per-target policy, scanning and tracing, and ingests OCSF AI events from AWS Security Lake (2026-11-23T23:07:52Z, stable). Everything else in-window is a patch train, a hardening bundle, or a consumer launch; those are one-line mentions below.

The Academy signal is the same lesson three ways. Judge error rates measured once do not transport across agents whose trajectories differ (2611.30742, Table 1). Agents fabricate most when a tool returns plausible partial data they ingest without noticing, not when a tool hard-denies (2611.28341, Tables 4–6). And text-to-SQL fan-out is fixed not by "can have many" dependency labels but by stating each measure's grain and per-key repetition counts (2611.29009, Table 2). For BQAA: audit with randomized human labels, flag partial payloads in traces, and store grain/multiplicity on the query record.

1. Transport-Safe Evaluation of LLM Agents: Trajectory-Dependent Judge Error and Anytime-Valid Auditing — full PDF read

Ameur, Bouzefrane & Banerjee (Efrei/Cnam; author's accepted version for ICAART 2027). Full PDF read: every part read in chunks, including Sections 3–6 methods/results, Section 7 proof sketches, Section 9 limitations, and references.

Claim: correcting judged success rates with one sensitivity/specificity pair assumes judge error is agent-invariant; when error depends on trajectory covariates (length, task family, tool-call intensity) and agents induce different trajectory mixes, the transported Rogan–Gladen correction has exact asymptotic bias (Theorem 1, Eq. 2) and reverses ranking exactly when the bias difference exceeds the true ability gap.

Key numbers (Table 1, four-agent semi-synthetic benchmark, 250 replications): scalar plug-in with oracle calibration: MAE 0.168, maximum bias 0.245, mean pointwise coverage 0.016 (nominal 0.95), exact full ranking 0/250. Uniform-audit and design-aware AIPW: maximum bias below 0.003, coverage 0.939/0.950. In the 48-cell stress test (Section 4.1), effective target support rises only 0.209 → 0.805 as calibration grows 600 → 4,800 labels, while 12%-audit AIPW holds MAE 0.018–0.020. Anytime-valid e-process (Section 5.3, Theorem 4): across 200 streams to t=3,000, repeatedly checked Wald intervals jointly cover 0.535 vs confidence sequence 0.995 (wider). Rank sets (Section 6): midpoint rankings recover exactly 0/1,000 under alternating displacement, while the identification-robust set covers 1.000. Motivating AgentRewardBench figures (sensitivity 0.84/0.88/0.88 short tercile → 0.18/0.36/0.48 long/capped) are explicitly prior analysis, not rerun evidence (Section 1).

BQAA use: store trajectory covariates with every judge verdict; budget a randomized human audit (known inclusion probabilities, positivity floor) instead of enlarging a single calibration set; publish rank sets, not point orderings, when support fails. Limits (Section 9): semi-synthetic main results, public-data analyses not rerun, expert labels treated as reference, stable-target assumption excludes agent updates/clustered tasks.

2. When Tools Fail, Agents Fabricate: Silent Failure and Verification-First Oversight in Tool-Using LLM Agents — full PDF read

Kaynar, Yan & Kingsford (CMU; NeurIPS 2026 workshop). Full PDF read: every part read in chunks, including Sections 3–6 (testbed, Monitor, setup, results), Section 7 limitations, and Appendix A prompts. Testbed RetryRealistic: real OS filesystem/SQLite/mock HTTP/constrained shell, 8 tools, 9 fault types with persistence semantics (Table 2), 30 tasks, 4 agents, 2,100 faulted runs (Section 5).

Key numbers: pooled baseline 45.6% correct, 17.3% fabricated, 37.1% honest-failure (Table 7); per-model correct 18.9–67.6%, fabricated 5.5–25.9% (Table 3). By fault: partial success fabricates at 38.3%; permission-denied honest-fails at 78.1% (Table 4). Mechanism "silent ingestion": on partial-data faults, 554/609 runs repeated the faulted call at most once and 27.8% of those fabricated (Section 6.3); payload-only sanitization: partial 35.2% fabricated vs denial 16.0% (Table 6). Verification-first Monitor (code grounding/surface/recompute S1–S3 + LLM drift S4, corrective directives not hard blocks): correct 45.6% → 56.3%, fabricated 17.3% → 10.8% (Table 7); weakest on partial success (38.3% → 32.1%) because truncated answers are grounded in rows actually received. Removing chain-of-thought: fabricated 17.3% → 25.6% (+8.3pp). Method note: LLM behavioral taxonomy κ=0.58 vs answer-vs-ground-truth grading κ=0.91 (Section 5) — prefer the objective metric.

BQAA use: add a trace flag for "first tool result accepted with no retry/verification" and a payload-completeness field; score fabrication separately from honest failure. Limits (Section 7): constrained shell/mock HTTP, scalar-only recompute, strong proprietary Monitor/judge models.

3. Mind the Trap As LLMs Guess the Data Model: Grain-Aware Guards Against Fan-out in Text-to-SQL and Its Benchmarks — full PDF read

Mahran (Incorta). Full PDF read: every part read in chunks, including Sections 2–8 (grain condition, BIRD audit, GrainTraps, evidence arms, results, limitations) and Appendices A–E (prompts, per-model Tables 3–5, checks, full Tables 6–12).

Claim: a duplicate-sensitive aggregate is correct iff each entity at its measure's grain appears once in the relation it reads (Proposition 2.1, grain condition); the two schema-invisible inputs — measure grain G and join-induced multiplicity μ — are computable from data. Audit findings (Section 4): fan-out distorts 4.2% of BIRD dev gold (median distorted answer 6.3× corrected where scalar); BIRD's independent cleaned release changed 44% of definitely-flagged gold vs 16% unflagged; releases BIRD-FanOut (486 reviewed questions) and GrainTraps (59 synthetic questions, six traps). Controlled study (8 LLMs, 7 arms, 38,232 runs): plain FD labels add nothing over per-column statistics on GrainTraps; grain-aware FDs (measure grain + per-key repetition counts + NULL-aware FDs) cut BIRD-train fan-out errors 18.4% → 13.1–13.6% (Table 2; 3.4–3.9 points beyond statistics, p<.001; holds on 183 held-out questions and under judge-free metrics, Figure 10/Table 10), while accuracy rises only ~3 points because hints often prescribe the over-count (127/229 distorted questions; Section 6.4) and most remaining errors are not fan-out. Audit as offline verifier (Section 6.7): flags 70–75% of distorted-gold reproductions, ~1% of correct dev answers, but 53% of correct answers on intended-fan-out questions — it detects repetition, not intent.

BQAA use: for data agents, attach measure grain + repetition-per-key to schema context and run a data-grounded fan-out audit on submitted SQL before presenting aggregates. Limits (Section 7/8): one benchmark family, SQLite only, LLM-judged labels/gold review (no human labeling), design-set contamination disclosed with held-out mitigation, evidence cost up to ~2× input tokens (Section 6.8).
