1. NEW rank: Continual Search — root-cause attribution over long-horizon agent traces is a search problem: an agentic judge prompted against its unread evidence (coverage 70.8%→97.4%) lifts GPT-5.5 attribution F1 0.349→0.498; honestly bounded by short-trace regressions and 2–3× cost

Root-Cause Attribution Is a Search Problem: Continual Search for Long-Horizon Agent Failures, Harsh Raj, David Lee, Anas Mahmoud, Renxiong Wang, Razvan-Gabriel Dumitru, Chenguang Wang, Tong Zhao, Yunzhong He, Darvin Yi, Vipul Gupta (Scale AI). v1 2026-09-11; no v2. Full text read (30 pp) including appendices (evidence-coverage ablation, stability runs, verbatim continuation prompts, TRAIL scoring notes). (PDF)

Automated RCA over massive agent execution logs fails because failure evidence is sparse, distant, and temporally disconnected from the terminal error — and one-shot LLM judges settle early on a superficially plausible diagnosis, leaving the true root-cause evidence uninspected. Continual Search runs the judge as an agent (Claude Agent SDK; Bash/Grep/Glob/Read over the trace artifacts) for four turns, each turn prompted against unread artifacts, unexplored spans, or unconsidered candidate steps (verbatim prompts in App. B.2); a Passive Continuation control ("reconfirm your label") isolates the instruction variable. Verified findings:

  • Headline, exactly (Table 2, MegaRCA-Mix): GPT-5.5 judge F1 0.349 → 0.498 at turn 4 (+42.7%; passive 0.401); Opus-4.8 0.478 → 0.620 (passive 0.559). TRAIL: GPT-5.5 weighted F1 0.426 → 0.500, Joint Accuracy 0.156 → 0.257. TELBench span accuracy 0.121 → 0.212.
  • The mechanism is evidence coverage, not vibes (App. A.1): artifact coverage 70.8% → 97.4% under Continual Search vs a 77.0% plateau under passive continuation; deduped observation tokens grow 42K→58K vs 48K. The judge literally had not read the decisive spans in the single-turn baseline.
  • Cheaper than the right baseline (Table 4): beats self-consistency (4-sample majority vote) and a heterogeneous 4-model judge panel on both TRAIL metrics, at $6.24 vs $8.66 per trajectory (GPT-5.5); ~2–3× single-turn cost ($2.11→$6.24), up to ~$15/trajectory on MegaRCA-Mix.
  • Lower-tier + search beats unguided higher-tier (Fig 4, §4.2): Sonnet-5 + Continual Search 0.518 WF1 > Opus-4.8 single-turn 0.486 (same family); GLM-4.7 + CS 0.473 > GPT-5.5 single-turn 0.426. The paper's own framing is within-family; the cross-model comparison is derivable from its tables, not a headline experiment.
  • Honest negative result (Table 3): on short traces Continual Search hurts — AgentRx 0.345→0.241, Who&When 0.548→0.452. The method is explicitly for long-horizon logs; deploy behind a length gate.
  • Stability (App. A.2): four independent Opus-4.8 TRAIL runs — CS beats passive in all four, WF1 gains 0.021–0.064. Bonus application (§6): the same machinery turns a taxonomy classifier into a benchmark-defect auditor, F1 0.16→0.74 vs purpose-built ABA 0.63.
  • Benchmark (Table 1, §3.2): MegaRCA-Mix = 50 human-annotated failure trials from Harbor Index, median ~286K tokens / 1.05 MiB per log, spanning GAIA, SWE-Bench, HLE, OpenRCA + 15 more; annotated for first consequential error; traces generated by Claude Code with Opus-4.8 and GPT-5.5.

Mandatory qualifications: n=50 flagship benchmark with no significance tests/CIs and conceded label noise over 286K-token logs; TRAIL Joint Accuracy is recall-only and the CS prompts say "add any new errors you find" — part of that metric's gain is mechanically recall-inflated (weighted F1 is the cleaner read); the TELBench turn-2 prompt expands the rubric mid-session, conflating search with criteria change; judges and trace-generators are the same model families (self-judging unexamined), though final scoring is formulaic F1 against human labels, not LLM-graded; annotation protocol thin (no annotator count/IAA); no code/data release statement; industry lab, arXiv v1, unrefereed.

BQAA/ADK application: the most directly transferable paper this cycle. The core design — track which spans/artifacts the judge has actually read, and prompt against the unread set — maps cleanly onto a BigQuery/OTel span store (their TRAIL setup literally judges OpenTelemetry JSON), and artifact-coverage becomes a first-class audit metric for any BQAA trace-judging path: a single-pass RCA verdict over a >40K-token trace should ship with its evidence-coverage number attached, or be treated as a guess. The deployment gates are equally adoptable: length-gate multi-turn search (short traces regress), budget 2–3× per trajectory, and score against formulaic anchors rather than LLM-graded finals. The §6 benchmark-defect audit is a direct template for BQAA eval-pipeline hygiene.
