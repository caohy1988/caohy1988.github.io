Fable 5 brief: industry + academy → BQAA

For: Haiyuan Cao, BigQuery Agent Analytics As of: 2026-08-29, ~22:30 PT (0.5.1 on PyPI at 19:50 PT; #435 plan v4 comments through 20:21 PT) Sources: the six notes in this folder plus primary pages fetched tonight (arXiv abs, ACL Anthology, vendor blogs/docs). Every number below is from one of those. Where a note and a primary page disagreed, the primary page wins and the disagreement is called out.

Thesis. This summer the industry converged on one demo and one substrate fight. The demo is "an agent reads production traces, clusters failures, opens a PR" (LangSmith Engine, Arize Signal, Amplitude Eval Agents). The substrate fight is who owns the trace table (Databricks Unity Catalog, Langfuse v4's append-only observations table, Amplitude's shared user_id). BQAA already won the substrate on GCP — a customer-owned, partitioned, joinable agent_events with no session cap — and tonight's 0.5.1 shipped the eval primitives (EvalBench reader, 8 rubrics, golden matching at 0.92). What it does not have is a named loop over those primitives, so it loses the demo while winning the warehouse. Academia, meanwhile, says the demo is overclaimed: on natural long traces the best exact-root-step attribution is 24.1% (LongRCA), human annotators only agree 39.5% on the step, and even injected-error benchmarks top out at ~22% error-mode F1 (Who&When Pro). That is the strongest argument for exactly the #435 shape reviewers converged on — session-level, EvalBench-only, uncertainty-gated — and against any span-forensics claim. The highest-expected-value six weeks: name the cluster→dataset→re-score loop on 0.5.1 primitives, catch the dated demand from the OpenAI Evals sunset, add three session-level columns (deterministic loop/thrash view, failure_mode enum, responsible_role with confidence). Do not start the #435 clock.

Industry

1. LangSmith Engine

What it actually is. A scheduled in-platform agent that reads a LangSmith tracing project, clusters recurring failures into named issues, diagnoses them against a connected repo, and drafts PRs and dataset examples. It is a consumer of LangSmith's store, not a new store.

How it works. Launched May 2026 (per the 2026-08-25 post). The Aug 25 release: "over twice as well" on internal IssueBench for issue identification, "25% better" on Terminal-Bench for proposed-fix effectiveness, 60M+ traces scanned and 20,000+ issues since launch. Self-hosted: orchestration runs in the customer VPC and calls LangSmith Intelligence, a managed zero-data-retention inference service; per the notes, if LSI is down Engine errors rather than degrading. Slack alerts, automatic Linear tickets, automatic close of stale issues, tighter links to trace locations. Reduced Analysis lets teams scan fewer traces to cut LCU spend. Plus and Enterprise only, SaaS and self-hosted. Roadmap (not shipped): automated verification of proposed fixes against datasets; Engine-generated eval datasets from traces. Buyer: platform engineering on LangGraph/LangChain.

What people get wrong. "Self-hosted Engine keeps the loop in your VPC." Only orchestration does. Inference leaves for LSI, and the product hard-fails without it. Second, "Engine verifies its fixes." It does not — verification is the first roadmap item in its own post. The 2× and 25% figures are first-party (IssueBench is internal; Terminal-Bench is public but the delta is LangChain's measurement).

Judgment. Threat — on the demo, not on the store.

TL;DR for BQAA. BQAA has every primitive Engine composes: v_tool_error/v_llm_error, categorical_evaluator, the 0.5.1 failure_attribution rubric, match_golden_qa, the EvalBench reader. It has no command or documented recipe that turns them into a named issues table. Reduced Analysis is an admission that scanning is the cost center; a warehouse pre-filters in SQL before any model sees a trace, which is BQAA's structural edge and should be the headline. Do not build the PR half. Build the cluster→dataset half (see Industry thesis, move 1). #435's labeled corpus is the same artifact under a different name — but Engine ships issues without a literature taxonomy, which is the evidence that clustering can precede taxonomy.

2. LangSmith Tuned Evaluators / Perceived Error

What it actually is. A LangChain-owned, post-trained judge model attached to a tracing project; you rent the evaluator, they own prompt, weights, credentials, and inference. Perceived Error is the first one: it flags threads with evidence the user perceived a mistake.

How it works. Announced 2026-08-18. Signal: explicit (corrections, repeated requests) or inferred (contradictory responses, acknowledged mistakes, unresolved outcomes). Eligibility: at least two human–AI message pairs and a configured idle period; result attaches as feedback "within 12 hours after the thread becomes eligible." Cost claim: 82% lower eval cost than frontier models on their benchmark, up to 98% in some early-partner workloads; "outperformed every frontier model" on LangChain's own benchmark. Docs: beta, threads only ("cannot run on individual runs"), organization-admin enablement, billed in LCUs only when feedback attaches (skipped/failed not billed), evaluated traces get extended retention and therefore extra storage charges. Blog: US Plus and Cloud Enterprise. Design partner Vanta.

What people get wrong. "This is online scoring." It is a batch job over completed, idle threads with a 12-hour lag. Mechanically it is a GraderPipeline run triggered by idleness, packaged as a product with a cheaper model behind it. The novelty is the cost curve and the zero-config attachment, not the architecture.

Judgment. Peer on mechanism, threat on cost narrative.

TL;DR for BQAA. Perceived Error is a session-level rubric; 0.5.1's response_usefulness (with declined) and first_time_right cover overlapping signal. BQAA's equivalent runs in-warehouse via AI.GENERATE with no egress — the missing piece is the trigger. A scheduled query that scores sessions once they go idle, writing to the same scores table, is the product-shaped gap; GraderPipeline as a manual batch is the demo-shaped gap. The extended-retention billing detail is a reminder that SaaS judges make traces more expensive to keep; BQ retention economics is the counter-line.

3. Arize Signal + managed agents

What it actually is. A scheduled worker over an AX tracing project that groups recurring failures, writes an investigation (evidence, likely cause, proposed fix), and — on Enterprise — hands a managed agent a repo to open PRs.

How it works. GA 2026-07-29. Caps: Free 10 issues/month, Pro 25, Enterprise unlimited + repo-backed PRs. Managed agents Enterprise-only, no additional charge through 2026-09-15. Same day: BYO Anthropic key, MANAGED_AGENT_* RBAC roles, Slack. 2026-07-27: remote evaluators GA on Enterprise (you host an HTTP endpoint; AX queries span data). 2026-07-21: Agent Swarms fleet view (Signal issues across projects by severity) and seven trajectory/session evaluators (Goal Completion, Path Efficiency, Reasoning Coherence, Session Resolution, Topic Coherence, Session Frustration, Session Completion). 2026-07-01: typed OpenInference columns (llm.system, llm.finish_reason, tool.id, embedding.invocation_parameters). Dogfood (Alyx post): 34 issues in 30 days, including a 227-second, 192-span invocation with 43 calls to get_datasets whose root span stayed OK. Data model: OpenInference span kinds in Arize's adb. Claimed warehouse sync to BigQuery/Databricks/Snowflake was not confirmed on any fetched Arize docs page.

What people get wrong. "Signal finds errors." The most-cited Signal win was a trace where every span status was OK. The failure was structural — repetition — not status='ERROR'. Any BQAA analysis keyed on status or the error views misses the class of failure Arize is selling.

Judgment. Threat on the demo; reference product for the category. The Sep 15 promo end is a calendar event.

TL;DR for BQAA. The 43× loop is one SQL statement on agent_events: group TOOL_STARTING by invocation_id, tool name, and a hash of the args, HAVING COUNT(*) > k. Ship it as an auto view; it costs nothing and is the single most convincing "warehouse beats APM" artifact. Arize's typed OpenInference columns are the analog of promoting hot attributes JSON keys to columns — worth doing for model and token counts, which every dashboard hits. Remote evaluators (customer-hosted HTTP judge) is the shape a Cloud Run judge already has; document it as such.

4. Langfuse v4

What it actually is. A rewrite of Langfuse's storage to an immutable, append-only observations table on ClickHouse, with user_id/session_id stamped on every row at ingest, replacing mutable traces+observations tables and read-time dedup.

How it works. GA 2026-08-17. Cloud becomes v4-only on 2026-11-16; self-hosted v3 gets security patches through January 2027. SDK floor: Python ≥ 4.7.0, JS ≥ 5.4.0. Per the notes: events_full plus a query-optimized events_core materialized view; self-host needs ClickHouse ≥ 25.12 (26.4 recommended); migration modes legacy / dual / events_only. Claims: table loads seconds→milliseconds; dashboards "at least 10x faster in large projects." Shipped alongside: Monitors (cost/quality/latency thresholds → Slack/webhook/GitHub Actions), code evaluators (Python/TS on live observations), full-text search, Pulse, Langfuse Assistant, Observations API v2, Metrics API v2. Context: ClickHouse acquired Langfuse 2026-01-16. Ingest is OTLP HTTP only (no gRPC), header x-langfuse-ingestion-version: 4. No Engine/Signal-class fixer shipped.

What people get wrong. "Langfuse is the open-source alternative to a warehouse." It is the open-source alternative to LangSmith. Its v4 data model just converged on agent_events: one denormalized append-only event row, partitioned, session and user on every row, no joins at read time. Langfuse spent a major version becoming a table. That validates the BQAA shape; it does not compete with it on joins or retention.

Judgment. Peer. Nov 16 is a calendar event.

TL;DR for BQAA. Pitch on joins and retention, not query latency — ClickHouse will win a latency benchmark. Where BQAA is actually behind: Monitors and code evaluators on live observations are product surfaces; BQAA's GraderPipeline is batch and has no alerting story beyond "write a scheduled query." The Nov 16 cutover forces self-host v3 teams to choose between a ClickHouse upgrade and re-platforming; a documented "land Langfuse exports in agent_events" path would be timed to that decision, but it is lower EV than the monitor recipe.

5. Amplitude Agent Analytics

What it actually is. Product analytics on agent conversations: each turn decomposes into Amplitude events (user message, tool call, agent response, session end) under the same user_id as product events, so funnels, cohorts, and retention run across agent quality and business outcome.

How it works. GA 2026-08-20 on every plan including free. Seven built-in Signals (task completion, response quality, user intent, session safety, user friction, negative feedback, data quality) with written rationale on [Agent] Session Record; topic clustering; custom LLM evaluators with calibration runs and BYOK; Eval Agents that watch topic spikes, emerging topics, tool reliability, evaluator failure modes, and signals, and can open Linear tickets, post to Slack, or send a Cursor PR. Ingest: OTel GenAI, AI SDK (Node/Python), HTTP [Agent] events, warehouse import. Caps: 5k / 5k / 10k / 20k agent sessions per month (Starter / Plus / Growth / Enterprise); chat retained 14 / 14 / 30 / 30 days. Performance: session queries "up to 400x faster"; enrichment-status filter 39.7s → sub-second. Customers: The Economist Lens 96.9% task success and −84% weekly task failures; Cashbook: bad first session → −15% retention. 2026-08-28 privacy post: modes full (text with default PII redaction of email/phone/card/SSN/IP), metadata_only (tokens/latency/model/cost only), customer_enriched (your pre-scored summaries only); three RBAC gates; tool schemas sent as a stable hash; attachments as metadata never bytes; seven session-safety classes (Normal, Off Topic, Prompt Injection, Abuse, Probing, Unsafe Output, Data Leak); scrubbing patterns open-sourced (Amplitude-AI-Node/.../privacy.ts). Core argument: standalone observability tools cannot correlate session quality with retention because they lack a shared user identity layer.

What people get wrong. Two beliefs, one lazy and one dangerous. Lazy: "Amplitude is an observability competitor." It is not; different buyer (PM/growth), different question ("did quality move retention?"). Dangerous: "BQAA already does the business join, so this is not a threat." BQAA can do the join. Amplitude does the join, with identity resolution, cohorts, a UI, and a privacy contract, on the free plan. The join is BQAA's pitch sentence and Amplitude's shipped product.

Judgment. Threat to the PM buyer and to the phrase "agent analytics"; distractor for the engineering roadmap.

TL;DR for BQAA. user_id is already a cluster key on agent_events. What is missing is one documented cohort recipe — sessions with a failing rubric score → 30-day retention from a product table — and a PM-grade surface to show it on. Today that surface is broken: Looker Studio #445 is closed but external access is attested FAILING; Grafana works and is not a PM tool. The 20k-session Enterprise cap and 30-day chat retention are the sales line: BQAA has no cap and retention is a BQ policy. On privacy: metadata_only ≈ payload_column_denylist, customer_enriched ≈ "bring your GraderPipeline scores" — the parity exists in Python; Java still does not redact secret: state, and Amplitude just made the redaction contract a procurement question.

6. Databricks OTel → Unity Catalog / Zerobus

What it actually is. A managed OTLP endpoint (Zerobus Ingest) that writes spans, logs, and metrics into pre-created Unity Catalog Delta tables; MLflow provisions the tables and views and remains the UI.

How it works. Docs last updated 2026-08-19. gRPC and OTLP/HTTP Protobuf only — application/json is rejected. Header x-databricks-zerobus-table-name selects the target; traces, logs, and metrics go to separate tables; tables must be provisioned first (the service does not create or alter schemas). Default quota 10,000 requests/second (the product blog's FAQ still says 200 QPS — the docs are newer). No storage cap. MLflow tables: _otel_spans, _otel_logs, _otel_metrics, _otel_annotations, plus _trace_unified / _trace_metadata views, liquid-clustered. Scorers run via SQL warehouse against UC. PII: the docs say nothing; the notes' reading is UC column masks and row filters. Beta as of 2026-04-09 per a Databricks PM post. Knowledge Assistant / Supervisor Agent not supported with UC traces. Named production users: Experian, Superhuman/Grammarly, Smartsheet, The Standard.

What people get wrong. "Databricks stores agent events the way BQAA does." It stores raw OTel spans. That means its table schema inherits the 0-of-63-stable convention problem directly: a gen_ai.* rename is a column rename in _otel_spans. BQAA's agent_events is an ADK event schema with OTel IDs as a join key — insulated from the vocabulary churn by design.

Judgment. Peer. The only warehouse-native peer.

TL;DR for BQAA. The gap is ingest breadth, not throughput. Databricks accepts any OTLP producer; BQAA's OTLP receiver allowlists ("claude_code", "codex"). "Any agent, anywhere" is their line and BQAA cannot say it. The fix is a generic producer with provenance/auth, which is exactly what #435 plan v4 calls wave 2 — keep it there, but it is the Databricks-parity item, not a forensics item. Do not chase 10,000 rps; Storage Write API is not the bottleneck, the allowlist is. Steal the _trace_unified idea: one documented view that presents an invocation as a single row.

7. OpenTelemetry GenAI semantic conventions

What it actually is. The shared attribute vocabulary for LLM and agent spans (gen_ai.*), now in its own repo, still at Development stability with no tagged release.

How it works. Repo open-telemetry/semantic-conventions-genai created 2026-05-05. Core semconv v1.42.0 (2026-06-12) deprecated the entire gen_ai namespace in the main repo; v1.43.0 (Jul 3) and v1.44.0 (Aug 4) ship none. Particula audit 2026-08-13: 63 gen_ai attribute keys, 0 stable, all development; zero tagged releases; README schema URL still TODO. TrueFoundry re-check 2026-08-21: unchanged. The production-breaking event is the Python package flip: deprecated opentelemetry-instrumentation-openai-v2 emitted v1.30.0-era names unless OTEL_SEMCONV_STABILITY_OPT_IN=gen_ai_latest_experimental; replacement opentelemetry-instrumentation-genai-openai 1.0b0 (2026-07-09) emits latest experimental unconditionally with no opt-out. Five breaking changes queued unreleased as of Aug 13: gen_ai.request.top_k double→int, gen_ai.agent.id scope removed from internal spans, gen_ai.system_instructions limited to text parts, gen_ai.provider.name no longer required on invoke_agent, agent/tool attribute removals. Historical renames: gen_ai.system → gen_ai.provider.name (v1.37.0, Aug 2025); prompt/completion tokens → input/output tokens (v1.27.0). gen_ai.evaluation.result event since v1.38.0 (Oct 2025). MCP: SEP-414 puts traceparent/tracestate/baggage in _meta.

What people get wrong. "OTel-native means interoperable." Datadog, Amplitude, Databricks, Weave, AgentCore, and ADK all say "OTel GenAI" and do not agree on field names. A dashboard keyed on gen_ai.system or gen_ai.usage.prompt_tokens goes silently blank on a package upgrade. Everyone is marketing a Development convention with no pin.

Judgment. Calendar event with no date; distractor if chased; peer on IDs only.

TL;DR for BQAA. The plugin's decision to not export OTel spans and to join on trace_id (with enable_otel_correlation as best-effort) is correct and should be defended in docs, not apologized for. Adopt exactly one thing: the shape of gen_ai.evaluation.result (name, score.value/score.label, explanation, response id) for the scores table — it is the only near-agreement on how a score attaches to a trace. For the OTLP receiver: COALESCE old/new token and provider names in views, never sum both, and add a contract test that asserts exact attribute keys so a rename fails CI instead of blanking Grafana.

8. OpenAI Evals sunset

What it actually is. OpenAI is retiring its hosted Evals product and pointing users to Promptfoo.

How it works. Deprecation announced 2026-06-03. Existing evals read-only 2026-10-31. Dashboard and API shut down 2026-11-30. Documented path: "Download runnable Promptfoo config" from a completed run, plus optional JSONL result import. Graders in eval workflows are in scope. Same-day deprecations: reusable prompts (v1/prompts) and Agent Builder, both ending Nov 30; new fine-tuning job restrictions from May 7. Per the notes, the Assistants API already sunset 2026-08-26. Agents SDK tracing (free dashboard, proprietary exporter, unavailable under ZDR) is unaffected.

What people get wrong. "Nov 30 is the deadline." Oct 31 is the operative date: after read-only you can still export, but you can no longer run, so any migration validation — re-run an old eval on the new harness and compare — must finish before Oct 31 or there is no fresh baseline to compare against.

Judgment. Calendar event. Demand shock for every evals-on-traces product, GraderPipeline included.

TL;DR for BQAA. One short migration guide with a worked example, published before Oct 31: OpenAI eval JSONL → BQ table → GraderPipeline with the 0.5.1 rubrics, match_golden_qa (threshold 0.92) as the answer matcher, results joined to agent_events on trace_id. Promptfoo is the code-first path; BQAA is the warehouse path for teams that want eval results next to production traces. Cheap, dated, and nobody else on GCP is going to write it.

9. Amazon Bedrock AgentCore unified log group

What it actually is. Plumbing: one CloudWatch log group per agent instead of spans in shared aws/spans and prompts elsewhere.

How it works. Announced 2026-07-23; default for runtimes created on/after 2026-07-20. Group /aws/bedrock-agentcore/runtimes/<agent_id>-<endpoint_name> holds traces, prompts, structured logs, stdout. Existing agents opt in with UNIFIED_TRACES_DESTINATION_ENABLED=true and ADOT ≥ 0.17.1 (configure docs cite 0.18.0). CloudWatch Transaction Search must be on or the UI is empty. Enables per-agent IAM and CMK. No judges, no clustering, no PR loop.

What people get wrong. "AgentCore observability is an analytics product." It is a log destination with a nicer default. SQL requires export (Logs → S3 → Athena).

Judgment. Distractor.

TL;DR for BQAA. Relevant only as a possible OTLP source (ADOT + OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT can fan out), which the BQAA allowlist blocks anyway. Nothing to do; do not write a comparison page.

10. APM camp (Datadog / New Relic / Elastic)

What it actually is. Infra APM vendors adding agent spans to the same pane as service traces.

How it works. Datadog renamed LLM Observability to Agent Observability; natively ingests OTel GenAI v1.37+ and OpenInference with a name-mapping table; Patterns (hierarchical topic clustering), Insights (anomaly on duration/error/evals), Bits Eval and Agent Console announced at DASH 2026-06-09/10; MCP toolset llmobs and skills for Cursor/Claude Code/Codex; not on Gov sites. New Relic AI Agent Monitoring: preview 2026-02-24, agent service map and drill-down, "no incremental spend" claim, LangGraph/AutoGen/Strands/Bedrock Converse. Elastic: AgentCore and Azure AI Foundry as first-class integrations; ES|QL, not a warehouse.

What people get wrong. "APM will take the analytics job." They win time-to-root-cause on one request and full-stack correlation. They lose long retention economics, arbitrary joins, and customer-owned SQL. Dual-export (OTel → Datadog/Cloud Trace; events → BQ) is the honest architecture and the one Google customers already run.

Judgment. Peer on capture, distractor on analytics.

TL;DR for BQAA. Do not compete on waterfalls; Cloud Trace already covers that via the trace_id join. The one pattern worth copying is Datadog's MCP toolset for coding agents: BQAA's skill repo (bigquery_agent_analytics_skill) has not been pushed since 2026-05-04 and its description still says "temp folder." That is the cheapest visible fix in this whole brief.

Industry thesis for BQAA

Four jobs are being sold this quarter:

| Job | Who ships it | BQAA today | |---|---|---| | Trace → issue → PR | LangSmith Engine, Arize Signal, Amplitude Eval Agents | Primitives only (categorical_evaluator, rubrics, golden matching, EvalBench reader). No named loop. | | Warehouse store of record | BQAA, Databricks/UC; Langfuse v4 converging on the same row shape | Wins on GCP: customer-owned table, no session caps, joins, ObjectRef multimodal, ADK 2.0 events OTel cannot model, Cloud Trace join. | | PM analytics on a shared user_id | Amplitude (GA, free plan, privacy contract) | user_id on every row, no cohort recipe, PM surface FAILING (#445). | | Surviving unstable OTel | Everyone exposed; Databricks most (raw spans as columns) | Insulated: ADK schema, IDs only, no span export. |

What BQAA uniquely wins. Jobs 2 and 4. Nobody else on GCP lands agent events as governed rows next to billing and CRM with an open-source capture path and no per-seat trace tax. Langfuse v4 is independent evidence that the append-only, session-stamped event row is the right physical model.

What it is losing the demo on. Job 1 entirely, and job 3 by default. There is no bqaa command that produces a table of named issues. Scores are not "on the trace" as a product surface. There is no monitor/alert story. The PM dashboard is attested broken. The OTLP allowlist means "any agent anywhere" is a sentence Databricks can say and BQAA cannot.

Three highest-EV moves in the next six weeks (through ~Oct 10), tied to 0.5.1 and #435:

  1. Name the loop, session-level, on 0.5.1 primitives. A documented, one-command recipe: deterministic clustering in SQL (tool-repeat loops, error signatures, failure_attribution rubric output) → an issues table keyed to invocation_id/trace_id → match_golden_qa to seed a golden set → GraderPipeline re-score. That is the cluster→dataset half of Engine; skip the PR half. This produces the labeled corpus #435 says is missing, as a side effect of a shipping feature, without starting #435. It is also the artifact that answers "Signal found a 43× loop with all spans OK" with a GROUP BY.
  2. OpenAI Evals → GraderPipeline migration guide, before Oct 31. JSONL importer, worked example, golden matching as the answer comparator. Dated demand, zero competition on GCP, small.
  3. Scores on the row + an idle-triggered monitor recipe. Make categorical_results / rubric output the canonical scores join on (trace_id, invocation_id) with gen_ai.evaluation.result-shaped columns, and document one scheduled query that scores newly idle sessions and alerts on threshold (the Tuned-Evaluator / Langfuse-Monitor analog). This is what the #435 P1 "reuse categorical_results instead of a 4-col parallel table" already asks for, so it is aligned rather than additive.

Not on the list, deliberately: widening the OTLP allowlist (wave 2 in #435 v4; Databricks parity, not urgent), a PR bot, a Looker Studio fix (blocked on an unowned external-access canary; Grafana is the demo until a dated out-of-domain check passes).

Academy

Tier 1

Reasoning Provenance for Autonomous AI Agents (AER) — Vispute & Kadam, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, arXiv 2603.21692

Claim. Computational state persistence (checkpoints) and execution traces are the mechanical layer; they cannot in general reconstruct reasoning provenance — intent, observation, inference — so population-level behavioral analytics needs those as first-class queryable fields on every step.

Mechanism. v1 2026-03-23, v2 2026-04-10. The Agent Execution Record: envelope.json (identity, IAM delegation chain, RAG chunk IDs), plans.jsonl (versioned plans with a revision_trigger step), steps.jsonl (intent, tool_calls, observation, inference, tokens), verdict.json (structured RCA category, confidence, evidence_chain, alternatives_rejected). Mock replay re-runs a new model/prompt against recorded tool outputs — a counterfactual regression suite with no live tools. Transport-layer interceptors (MCP stdio proxy, bash DEBUG trap, filesystem watcher) yield an independent fidelity score so self-reported reasoning is not trusted blindly. Storage claim (preliminary, from the notes): ~4–22× more compact than cumulative checkpoints. Deployed "preliminary" on a production platformized RCA agent.

Limit. No benchmark, no accuracy number; the authors call validation ongoing. Intent and inference remain self-report; the fidelity interceptors verify actions, not thoughts. Single-vendor, single-agent deployment.

Judgment. Steal the schema.

TL;DR for BQAA. agent_events has observation (TOOL_COMPLETED.content) and tool calls; it has no intent or inference field and no plan versioning. Cheapest adoption: optional attributes.reasoning.{intent, inference} keys on LLM_RESPONSE / TOOL_STARTING via the custom_metadata_allowlist, and treat ADK 2.0 AGENT_STATE_CHECKPOINT as the plan_revision hook. verdict.json is the output shape categorical_results should adopt for #435 (category, confidence, evidence_chain, alternatives_rejected) — it bakes in the "uncertainty must gate" P1. Mock replay is the mechanism behind #435's "counterfactual value gate": if tool outputs are stored, a judge or prompt change can be regressed against EvalBench traces without re-running tools.

UModel: An Agent-Ready Observability Data Modeling Method at Scale — Pei et al., CNIC/CAS, UCAS, Alibaba, Tsinghua, arXiv 2606.04799

Claim. RCA agents fail on siloed telemetry because they cannot write correct queries; model observability as objects (entities, datasets, links) with a pipeline query language and PaaS-level tools, and the agent traverses instead of generating SQL/PromQL.

Mechanism. Submitted 2026-06-03. EntitySet / DataSet / EntitySetLink / DataLink; U-SPL (Source | Command | …) with MetaSearch / USearch / GSearch (Cypher-capable graph calls). Production at Alibaba Cloud > 1 year: tens of thousands of users, millions of ops/s, sub-second queries. On an AIOps 2025 Challenge remodel, RCA location accuracy +8% (notes: +8.12%) vs a traditional model — and that baseline was handed 100% preloaded data because raw PromQL generation was < 5% accurate (the notes cite PromAssistant GPT-4-Turbo QueryAcc ≈ 2.6%). PaaS entity tools beat IaaS SPL-from-scratch by +21–27 points of location accuracy.

Limit. Infra RCA over microservices, not agent trajectories. +8% is modest. U-SPL is proprietary. The scale numbers are deployment facts, not experimental results.

Judgment. Steal the architecture — specifically the tool layer.

TL;DR for BQAA. Context Graph is the EntitySet; agent_events is the DataSet; the property-graph link tables are the Links. What is missing is the PaaS layer: get_trace_dag(trace_id), tool_error_summary(session), rubric_scores(invocation) as tools, not "here is agent_events, write SQL." UModel's < 5% query-accuracy number is the reason the stale skill repo matters. For #435, the judge/labeler must be fed entity tools and compressed observations, or it will hallucinate joins over a 15-column JSON table.

LongRCA Bench — Zhang, Feng, Pei et al., CNIC/CAS, Alibaba Tongyi, Tsinghua, SMU, arXiv 2608.15242

Claim. On natural, long failed trajectories, exact-root-step attribution is barely solved (24.1%) and even humans agree on the step only 39.5% of the time; responsible-role attribution is tractable (51.1%, human agreement 65.9%).

Mechanism. v1 2026-08-15, v3 2026-08-21. 1,140 natural (not injected) failed trajectories from SWE-bench Pro, Terminal-Bench 2, TravelPlanner, VitaBench, WebArena Verified; 178,137 steps; median 145 steps, max 728; median 48 steps after the labeled root. Labels: responsible role (a recorded workflow role, may be human) and earliest decisive unrepaired root step, scored independently. RCTA (training-free): segment → recall candidates from summaries → walk back X (-> Y) handoff instructions. 51.1% role / 24.1% exact step vs 13.2% for the best matched baseline (ECHO).

Limit. "Natural" means benchmark runs, not production. RCTA is their method on their benchmark. Five domains. A 65.9% role ceiling means role labels are themselves noisy.

Judgment. Gates #435.

TL;DR for BQAA. This paper decides what #435 may claim. Role-level attribution is defensible and agent_events.agent already carries the role → responsible_role is a session-level column. Exact root_step_idx is not defensible when human agreement is 39.5%; if it is ever added it needs a candidate set with confidence and a root_to_end_distance, not a scalar. EvalBench traces are short next to a 145-step median; a session-level MVP that passes there does not license span forensics. Evaluate RCA jobs on long natural traces separately from Who&When-style short ones.

Who&When Pro — Liu et al., Penn State / AG2ai / MBZUAI / Oregon State / Mathos, arXiv 2607.09996 (original Who&When: Zhang et al., ICML 2025, arXiv 2505.00212)

Claim. Even with 12,326 golden-labeled failures, frontier LLMs cannot reliably attribute: best text step accuracy 73.9%, error-mode F1 only ~22%, and accuracy collapses on long traces.

Mechanism. 2026-07-10. Warm-start injection: replay a successful prefix exactly, inject one taxonomy-conditioned error, continue — avoiding the pre-injection drift of re-rollout methods (AEGIS, AgenTracer). 26 source benchmarks, 15 frameworks, 3 modalities (text/image/video), 18 error modes across six families (perception, reasoning, planning, action, verification, coordination). Human review on 100 traces: 94% step / 90% agent / 90% mode, Fleiss κ 0.73. Traces > 12K tokens drop step accuracy from 94% to 50%. All-at-once beats step-by-step (early commitment) and is cheaper. Planning/verification/coordination errors are systematically misread as "reasoning." Giving the judge the ground-truth answer hurts reasoning-error diagnosis (outcome shortcut). Context: original Who&When — 127 systems, 184 failures, best 53.5% agent / 14.2% step.

Limit. Injected, single-error traces; labels are construction artifacts. LongRCA shows natural traces are much harder (24% vs 74% step). Multi-fault and orchestrator↔subagent dual-fault cases (DoVer) are not represented.

Judgment. Steal the enum; distrust the accuracy.

TL;DR for BQAA. failure_family (6) / failure_mode (18) is the enum for categorical_results in the #435 MVP — merge with MAST's 14 where they overlap rather than inventing a third. Judge-design rule: the process judge must not see the golden answer. That is directly relevant because 0.5.1's match_golden_qa gives GraderPipeline exactly that answer — keep correctness and failure_attribution in separate calls with separate inputs. Report any attribution accuracy against LongRCA-class lengths, never against injected sets.

AgentProcessBench — Fan et al., RUC et al., KDD 2026, arXiv 2603.14465

Claim. Step quality in tool-using agents needs ternary labels (+1 effective / 0 exploratory-neutral / −1 harmful); judges over-predict +1 and cannot separate 0 from −1; StepAcc overstates competence and FirstErrAcc is the honest metric.

Mechanism. v1 2026-03-15, v2 2026-06-01; KDD 2026 (Jeju, Aug 9–13), DOI 10.1145/3770855.3817494. 1,000 trajectories, 8,509 human-labeled steps; HotpotQA, GAIA, BFCL, τ²-Bench; five generator models; IAA 89.1% (κ 0.767); error-propagation labeling. Unsuccessful traces accumulate errors (HotpotQA: 64.6% incorrect steps on failures vs 14.0% on successes). Weaker models look step-accurate because they fail fast. Gemini-3-Flash-Thinking: 81.6% StepAcc but 65.8% FirstErrAcc. Process positivity plus outcome filtering improves Best-of-N; two-stage Final→Process is consistently best.

Limit. Short tool-use QA/API traces (≈8.5 steps on average), not long-horizon. Human labels on benchmark traces. Five generators, one annotation protocol.

Judgment. Steal the metric and the label scheme.

TL;DR for BQAA. FirstErrAcc is the SLO for any localization claim in #435 and should be the week-4 gate metric, not session-level agreement. Ternary is mandatory: without a neutral label the tool_usage rubric will punish healthy exploration. Reserve, do not yet populate, step_label ∈ {-1,0,+1}, first_error_idx, pos_step_ratio. Dashboard rule from the fail-fast finding: normalize any step-quality metric by trace length or short failures will look clean.

Beyond Black-Box Benchmarking (ABBench) — Moshkovich, Mulian, Zeltyn, Eder, Skarbovsky, Abitbol, IBM Research Israel, arXiv 2503.06745

Claim. Benchmark the analytics system, not the agent: input is a runtime log, output is discovered flows and issues. Mechanism. Submitted 2025-03-09. Analytics taxonomy: passive (insights, recs, task-flow DAGs), exploratory (RCA, comparison, retrieval), interventional (config tuning, task injection, component replacement). User study n=38: 79% agree non-determinism is a major challenge; per the notes 60% say current analytics don't meet needs, 77% struggle with RCA, 76% prioritize flow understanding. Flow variability via mean graph-edit distance across repeated runs (CV 63% on execution graphs). Limit. Preprint; the benchmark is small and IBM-internal in origin. Judgment. Context Graph plus the span DAG make GED and flow-clustering computable in SQL+graph; that is the #1 practitioner need in their study. TRAIL — Deshpande, Gangal, Mehta, Krishnan, Kannappan, Qian, Patronus AI, arXiv 2505.08638

Claim. LLMs are bad at localizing errors in real OTel/OpenInference traces. Mechanism. v1 2025-05-13. 148 human-annotated traces (notes: 1,987 spans, 841 errors) from GAIA and SWE-bench Lite; taxonomy reasoning/planning/execution including API 4xx/5xx, resource exhaustion, tool-definition errors; best model Gemini-2.5-Pro ≈ 11% joint accuracy. Traces run to hundreds of thousands of tokens. Limit. 148 traces; 2025 models. Judgment. Cite-and-ignore, except the scaling lesson. TL;DR for BQAA. agent_events already has TRAIL's operational half (TOOL_ERROR, LLM_ERROR, status, error_message). The lesson is job shape: judge spans or windows, never whole traces; sample in SQL first. TRAIL is the reason a single AI.GENERATE over a concatenated session is the wrong #435 baseline.

Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail? (MAST) — Cemri, Pan, et al., UC Berkeley, NeurIPS 2025 D&B, arXiv 2503.13657

Claim. MAS failures fall into 14 modes in 3 categories; they are architecture-specific and not fixed by prompts alone. Mechanism. 1,600+ annotated traces across 7 frameworks; taxonomy built on 150 traces; categories system design (41.8%), inter-agent misalignment (36.9%), task verification (21.3%); LLM-as-judge pipeline with κ = 0.88 per the abstract (the older research note says 0.77 — use the abstract); interventions +9–16 pp. Limit. Research MAS frameworks, not production; single-label per trace. Judgment. Steal the enum, merged with Who&When Pro. TL;DR for BQAA. Inter-agent misalignment (withholding, ignored input, conversation reset) is invisible to per-agent graders; it needs the joint trace, which is exactly what A2A_INTERACTION and AGENT_TRANSFER rows plus parent_span_id provide. A mast_mode column at session level fits the #435 MVP.

TRCA — Zhang, Chen, Zhou, Lv, Chang, Cui, Wu, Zhou, Beihang / Tsinghua / BAAI, arXiv 2608.16156

Claim. Sparse terminal rewards can be replaced by deterministic, environment-grounded per-transition rubrics. Mechanism. 2026-08-17. Rubrics Evidence / Execution / Invalidity per (s_t, a_t, s_{t+1}); Foundational signed local reward plus a Breakthrough reward paid only the first time a condition is covered; rubrics generated once per benchmark then rule-bound. Motivating stats: 96.5% of early rollouts fail, 85.6% of groups have no success anchor, 72.2% of failed-trajectory actions still carry signal. WebShop +6.0–12.6% (Qwen2.5-7B); SearchQA +1.9–18.3% (3B). Limit. RL training paper; rubrics are per-benchmark. Judgment. Steal the schema. TL;DR for BQAA. These flags are computable without an LLM: invalid_tool_rate (malformed/rejected/no-op tool calls from TOOL_ERROR and empty results), repeat_evidence_without_breakthrough (thrash), first_coverage_latency. As session-level aggregates they fit the MVP and are the deterministic half of move 1.

Graphectory — Liu, Chen, Krishna, Sinha, Ganhotra, Jabbarvand (UIUC / IBM), PACMPL OOPSLA 2026, arXiv 2512.02393; Viewer — Jyu, Liu, Jabbarvand, ASE 2026 tool track, arXiv 2608.17195

Claim. Coding-agent trajectories become analyzable when encoded as phase-aware graphs (localization → patch → validation → general). Mechanism. Parent v3 2026-04-10, DOI 10.1145/3798271; viewer submitted 2026-08-17. 4,000 SWE-agent/OpenHands runs on SWE-bench (3,973 nonempty); raw logs average 35,664 lines → graphs of 34.5 nodes / 49.6 edges with 9–28% dedup of repeated actions; analysis in under four minutes; online monitoring with rollback improves resolution 6.9–23.5%; the viewer's 5-person forensics study went from 8% to 84% task accuracy vs the SWE-agent CLI viewer. Limit. Coding agents only; phases are domain-specific. Judgment. Steal the schema (phase enum, deduped action node). TL;DR for BQAA. The Claude Code / Codex OTLP producers are exactly this corpus. A phase column and a phase-transition fact table over agent_events make loop detection and success-vs-fail Sankeys a Grafana panel, not a custom UI.

TRACE: Trajectory Reasoning through Adaptive Cross-Step Evidence Aggregation — Mittapalli et al. (UMass Amherst, Adobe Research, Dolby, U. Oregon, Cisco), arXiv 2606.07054

Claim. Long-trajectory monitoring needs targeted inspection with a persistent evidence store, not one pass over the whole trace. Mechanism. 2026-06-05. SHADE-Arena, 463 traces, avg 91.8 turns, 10 domains. Triage–Inspect–Judge loop (local and non-adjacent pattern windows; InspectStep / CompareSteps / CheckPolicy / AnalyzeDecisions). Claude Sonnet 4.6: F1 0.713, recall 0.844 vs full-trajectory 0.577 / 0.405 and sequential-chunk 0.700 / 0.580, at 12.3 vs 18.8 LLM calls. Failure mode: manipulation evasion 0/17 vs 58.8% sequential; high-recall monitors over-flag concentrated-signal tasks. Limit. Sabotage detection, not failure attribution; one benchmark. Judgment. Steal the job shape. TL;DR for BQAA. This is the template for any judge over agent_events: materialize suspect windows in SQL, persist evidence items with severity/confidence in a table, judge over the evidence log. Route by task class; one global suspicion UDF will fail on both ends.

AgentRx — Barke, Goyal, Khare, Singh, Nath, Bansal (Microsoft Research / Microsoft / UIUC), arXiv 2602.02475

Claim. Diagnosis should be constraint-driven and auditable: synthesize constraints, validate step by step, then judge from the violation log. Mechanism. 2026-02-02. 115 natural failures across τ-bench, Flash (incident-management MAS), and Magentic-One (from Who&When); 9 grounded-theory categories; 42.7 annotator-hours. Best config +23.6 pp localization / +22.9 pp category vs their taxonomy-aware baseline; τ-bench step index 32.2% → 54%. One-shot constraints win on short traces; step-by-step wins on ≤50-step Magentic traces because 16k-token contexts dilute one-shot. Limit. 115 traces; short by LongRCA standards. Judgment. Steal the schema (violations fact table). TL;DR for BQAA. Policy checks as views: constraint_id, taxonomy_target, verdict, evidence_window. The judge reads violations, not transcripts. The 0.5.1 scope_compliance rubric is a violation type, not a score, and should land in that table.

AgentPRM — Xi et al., Fudan / Ant Group, WWW 2026 (DOI 10.1145/3774904.3792551), arXiv 2511.08325

Claim. Agent steps are not true/false; score promise (proximity to goal) and progress (marginal contribution), labeled with TD + GAE, > 8× more compute-efficient than MC-rollout PRMs. Mechanism. DoVer — Ma, Zhang, Yang, Kang, Lin, Rajmohan, Zhang (CAS / Microsoft), ICLR 2026, arXiv 2512.06749

Claim. Attribution from logs is a hypothesis; validate it by intervening and replaying from a checkpoint. Mechanism. v3 2026-01-31; ICLR 2026 poster (OpenReview mrEK16Jy6h). Per the notes, 14/29 GAIA cases in Who&When had annotation uncertainty; GPT-4o step accuracy 44% on certain vs 24% on uncertain cases. Interventions flip 18–28% of Magentic-One failures on GAIA/AssistantBench and 49% on AG2/GSMPlus; validate or refute 30–60% of hypotheses; up to 16% milestone progress; per the notes 29–67% remain inconclusive because sub-agents cannot execute the edit; Self-Refine/CRITIC-style end-of-trace critique flipped 0%. Limit. Needs a replayable environment; inconclusive rates are high. Judgment. Gates #435. TL;DR for BQAA. This is the mechanism behind the #435 P1 "counterfactual value gate": a taxonomy label that cannot be validated by replay is a guess with a name. AGENT_STATE_CHECKPOINT is the hook; trial_id, replan_step, intervention, intervention_fulfilled are the columns. Report "% hypotheses validated," not "judge accuracy vs a single gold step."

Academic thesis for BQAA

Academia is ahead on WHAT to measure; industry is ahead on the WRITE PATH. BQAA sits in the gap. No paper above streams events into a warehouse at Storage Write API rates, and no vendor above publishes an error taxonomy with a human-agreement number. BQAA owns the write path and has, as of tonight, rubrics and golden matching on the consume side. The papers tell you which columns to put between them.

Schema stack. AER (intent / observation / inference + versioned plans + verdict shape) → UModel (entity graph + tool layer over the warehouse, not raw SQL) → AgentTrace (three surfaces; BQAA has two) → Wang et al. (evidence-support edges in the graph). Adopt in that order; the first two are cheap.

Failure stack. Who&When Pro's 18 modes ∪ MAST's 14 for the enum. LongRCA for the honesty: natural long traces give 24.1% exact step, 51.1% role, and human agreement of 39.5% / 65.9%. Injected-error benchmarks (Who&When Pro, AEGIS, AgenTracer) overstate judge accuracy; DoVer shows 14/29 of the original Who&When GAIA labels were themselves uncertain. AgentRx's violation log is the auditable middle layer.

Process stack. AgentProcessBench: ternary labels and FirstErrAcc (81.6% StepAcc vs 65.8% FirstErrAcc on the same model — the gap is the overclaim). TRCA: deterministic Evidence / Execution / Invalidity flags. AgentPRM: promise/progress later, if per-step state is retained.

Jobs stack. TRACE's triage–inspect–judge multi-pass; LongRCA's segment-then-walk-back-handoffs; DoVer's intervene-and-replay. One LLM call over a concatenated session is the wrong baseline for all three.

This gates #435. The session-level, EvalBench-only MVP is fine and is what the literature supports. Do not claim span forensics until traces of LongRCA length survive a FirstErrAcc gate. Do not start the clock — the P1s (counterfactual value gate, snapshot consistency in the EvalBench reader, categorical_results reuse, ~100-failure floor, uncertainty gating, D4 governance including prompt injection over concatenated traces) are each backed by a paper above, and the SANA characterization in the thread needs a re-read.

Three columns or jobs to add in the next six weeks, ranked by expected value against #435 scope:

  1. Deterministic loop/thrash view (v_tool_repeat or similar): repeated identical tool + args within an invocation, count, first/last span, plus TRCA-style invalid_tool_rate. No LLM, no labels, session-level, matches the single most-cited industry win (Arize's 43× loop with OK spans) and the Graphectory/TRCA findings. Ships in SDK views; zero overlap with #435's judge work. Highest EV per hour in this brief.
  2. failure_family / failure_mode enum in categorical_results (Who&When Pro 18 ∪ MAST 14), session-level, judge blind to the golden answer, with confidence and evidence_chain per AER's verdict shape. This is the #435 MVP's output column; defining it now costs nothing and does not start the clock.
  3. responsible_role + candidate set + uncertainty (LongRCA): role-level attribution is the defensible half; store a ranked candidate list with confidence rather than one label, and gate downstream aggregation on it (P1 "uncertainty must gate"). Explicitly not on the list: root_step_idx, first_error_idx, step_label — reserve the names, populate nothing until a FirstErrAcc gate passes on long natural traces.
