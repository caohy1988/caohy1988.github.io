Read this first

Three Tuesday-listing papers clear the bar, and they triangulate one BQAA question: can you prove what the agent actually did?

DataTrace (2611.18926) grades a data agent's root-cause answer and the evidence it inspected separately: seven proprietary models average 48% root-cause accuracy with an 18% mean conditional unsupported rate — correct answers with too little inspected evidence. Where the Evidence Lives (2611.20389) shows conventional trace redaction destroys repair (140/210 vs 210/210 fault-task success, Table III) while still leaking identity (225/240 uniquely identified under normalized matching), and that comparison-fact or execution-scoped-pseudonym exports preserve repair with zero identifier matches — until row counts re-identify 97/240 anyway. Auditable Authorization (2611.19237) audits human approval at model, harness and workflow layers: 7/300 paired approvals unfinished, CrewAI restoration omits 336/672 approvals, and 4/59 comparable public n8n workflows execute after Reject.

NEW 1 — DataTrace: Benchmarking Data Agents for Evidence-Grounded Root-Cause Localization — abs

Full PDF read (32 pages via pdftotext in chunks: §§1–7, Tables 1–7/S27–S32, Figs. 1–2, Appendices incl. operator library, sandbox transfer probe and training-pool analysis; methods/results/limitations included). Not in live catalog (grep 2611.18926: 0 hits).

DataTrace builds 83 runnable environments from five source families (BIRD-CRITIC, Spider2, dbt repos, mined incidents, synthetic) as typed provenance graphs, injects one of 21 fault operators across six families (§2), and scores root-cause correctness (RC@1) separately from evidence-path coverage (ER/EF1, §3.3), flagging correct-but-ungrounded answers (ER<0.5) as lucky guesses. Headline: seven proprietary models average 48% RC@1 with mean per-model conditional unsupported rate 18% (Abstract, Fig. 1d); the unified 896-episode leaderboard (Table S32) spans RC@1 0.195–0.688 with grounded RC@1g always lower. Grounding failure is structured by fault mechanism, dependency distance and modality — agents skip query plans and incidents (Fig. 1e) — and in-loop GRPO training on a matched held-out batch raises grounded-correct share 83.7%→94.6% and RC@1 29.2%→36.7% (Abstract; Table 4/S30 cross-domain CIs exclude zero). Sandbox probe caveat: on real 24–100-model Spider2 pipelines ER stays near floor and stronger backbones explore less (Table S28). Limitation (§7): the grounded flag measures coverage of one accepted witness path, not causal evidence use.

BQAA read: score diagnosis and inspected-evidence coverage as separate fields on every agent trace; an 18% unsupported-correct rate is exactly what answer-only evals hide. Fault-injection over a lineage graph is a reusable harness pattern for BigQuery pipeline agents.

NEW 2 — Where the Evidence Lives: Measuring Privacy and Diagnosability of Exported AI-Agent Traces — abs

Full PDF read (whole paper §§I–VI, Tables I–IV). Not in live catalog (grep 2611.20389: 0 hits).

Executable benchmark over 3 synthetic multi-tenant workflows, 7 fault families, 15 export policies and 240 final executions (§III-D): a diagnostic program proposes an edit from the exported trace alone, a trusted executor reruns, and output correctness is scored against a sealed reference. Regex/schema redaction: 140/210 fault-task success, 120/190 recovery, yet 225/240 (regex) / 221/240 (schema) uniquely identified under normalized matching (Table III, §IV-A) — redaction loses the ownership comparison and keeps encoded identifiers. Comparison facts and execution-scoped pseudonyms: 210/210 / 190/190 with 0 unique-ID / 0 exposure (Table III); an independently written program still gets 39/42 (facts) vs 42/42 (pseudonyms) (Table IV). But known-volume matching re-identifies 97/240 under every policy; removing seven counter fields from the facts export stops it (0/240) while preserving 210/210 (§IV-C). Scope (§VI): synthetic workflows, bounded edits, shared cells limit population inference.

BQAA read: trace export is a tested interface, not a redaction default — ship comparison facts or execution-scoped pseudonyms, audit counts/linkage separately, and verify repaired output with an independent diagnostic program before sharing traces across tenants.

NEW 3 — Auditable Authorization for LLM Agents: Empirical Evidence Across Model, Harness, and Workflow Layers — abs

Full PDF read (whole paper §§I–VIII, Tables III–IV, Figs. 3–4). Not in live catalog (grep 2611.19237: 0 hits).

Formalizes refusal safety + bounded authorization completeness and audits three boundaries (model call selection, harness scheduling/resume, workflow routing) with paired Approve/Reject decisions, exact traces and native business-state witnesses. Model layer: 7/300 paired approvals unfinished with zero denied effects. Harness layer: combined parallel approve-both replies lose one approved branch in 3/3 tasks per model (ADK 2.14.0 replay, §IV-B); CrewAI 1.15.37 restoration records approval but does not reschedule, omitting 336/672 approved effects while all 672 Rejects suppress (§IV-C; source-copy repair restores 672/672). Workflow layer: 4/59 policy-comparable public n8n workflows execute the protected effect after Reject (6.8%, Wilson 2.7–16.2%, Table IV). Deliverable is an audit contract + triage (Fig. 4): follow the first divergence between recorded decision and native effect. Scope (§VII): reproducible mechanisms, not deployment prevalence; AI-annotation κ 0.19–0.48 on semantics, native effects are the endpoint.

BQAA read: a human-approval gate is not a control until intent→call→dispatch→native-effect are each witnessed; log scoped decision + pending-work identity through pause/resume and parallel aggregation, and test Reject paths by native replay, not gate-presence checks.
