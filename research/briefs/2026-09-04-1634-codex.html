Academy

1. EquiReview-R — an investigation must be able to revise its own findings

arXiv:2609.03943 · September 3 · full PDF, 16 pages, §§3–7, especially Table 1.

On 271 previously unseen AI papers, EquiReview-R records major overcritique on 8.1% of papers versus 17.3% for a generation-only control with closely matched inference: 23.5 versus 23.7 calls per paper. Major omission is 12.5% versus 13.7%. The system revisits existing concerns against supporting and countervailing evidence before searching for more. Refuted, merged and resolved concerns leave the visible review while remaining in its history.

These are model-adjudicated outcomes. The judges are separate calls within the GPT-5.6 family; materiality agreement is 0.52, and domain-expert validation remains outstanding. The experiment uses main-paper evidence and a fixed external search procedure, so its omission rates do not establish scientific completeness.

BQAA + ADK judgment. Preserve a finding's identity, evidence, disposition and revision history. Test withdrawal of an unsupported allegation separately from remediation of a confirmed defect. A smaller issue count should be explainable from those transitions. This extends the existing feedback-correction test to the investigator's findings.

2. DECO — test whether the judge follows the selected criterion

arXiv:2609.03814 · September 3 · full PDF, 22 pages, §§3–5 and §7, especially Table 3.

The study holds content fixed and evaluates appearance, harmful intent and actionable facilitation separately. On the 20,000-example Civil Comments sample, GPT-5.2 correctly labels both intent/facilitation judgments in 99% of cases whose diagnostic targets agree, versus 29% where they differ. These are conditional subsets, not an overall accuracy decline. Pair accuracy requires both predictions to match their targets; merely changing the answer earns no credit.

Targets come from GPT-4.1 content-factor annotations plus heuristic criterion functions. Five humans checked a stratified 200-example subset, but the paper explicitly treats the targets as diagnostic approximations. Results depend on the criterion wording and its ambiguity.

BQAA + ADK judgment. Add paired rubric cases with identical evidence and independently adjudicated expected judgments. Include pairs that should agree and pairs that should differ. Retain criterion version and pair identity. This complements the catalogued response-reversal/rubric-artifact test by varying the criterion while holding the input fixed.
