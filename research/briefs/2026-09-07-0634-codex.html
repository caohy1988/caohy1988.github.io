Industry

I1. Inficy beta: show the origin and limits of execution evidence

NEW — product-level launch. JB's Inficy announcement carries publication metadata 2026-09-07T08:45:12Z, or 01:45:12 PT, inside this slot. The official product page describes a hosted execution record with ordered activity, failures, recovery and human intervention where the integration exposes them.

The consequential distinction is between runtime-observed records from supported integrations and agent-reported checkpoints from hosted ChatGPT or Grok Bot sessions. The latter are excluded from NexArt certification. For runtime-observed executions, Inficy presents the stored NexArt artifact as authoritative and the timeline as its readable projection, with export, verification and optional certification. These are documented product claims; this review did not execute a connector or independently test the verifier. Launch explanation, capture and verification.

Boundary: artifact verification and certification do not establish that the action was correct, safe, complete or compliant, or that uncaptured activity never happened. The current product pages say open beta, while the older security page still describes an invited pilot and reports no external security audit. Treat onboarding maturity as unresolved. The public plugin repository documents a connector, not a public implementation of the entire capture service.

BQAA TL;DR — inference: let reviewers see evidence origin and capture coverage alongside an outcome. An intact record of an agent's claim should not silently acquire the authority of an independently observed action. Test whether that distinction changes a reviewer's conclusion before adding a new attestation product.

Academy

A1. OR-Clarify / InterOPT: requirement recovery, readiness and question cost — arXiv:2609.05258

NEW. Sihan Ge et al., Ask Before You Optimize: Dynamic Pre-Formulation Clarification for Interactive Optimization. v1 submitted September 4; the PDF's September 7 dateline is not a new-version record. Full PDF read: 16 pages. PDF.

OR-Clarify evaluates 100 partial optimization requests, 178 hidden requirement slots, a fact-bounded simulated user and five runs per case. In the Choice protocol, InterOPT raises Core Exact recovery from MC-D's 0.506 to 0.675, while mean atomic questions rise 2.350 to 10.036. These endpoints use each method's stopping policy; Figure 4 supplies the complementary comparison at equal question counts. In Open interaction, InterOPT's 0.538 trails the adapted ORPilot's 0.583. Table 2 and Figure 4, PDF pp. 12–13.

Critical correction: the gap ledger changes Open → Asked, not verified-resolved. Open entries do not mechanically block a readiness declaration. The study evaluates elicitation before formulation, without establishing downstream solver success or real-user tolerance for extra questions. Sections 3–5.

BQAA TL;DR — inference: extend the existing requirements-to-acceptance fixture with separate records for question asked, answer received, requirement recovered and readiness declared. Compare recovery at a fixed question budget, then test the resulting service behavior. This supplies the upstream clarification diagnostic missing from a final pass/fail score; it complements the already-published τ^τ-Bench card without re-carding it.

A2. Enoki: connect a factual check to the unsupported answer span — arXiv:2609.00581

NEW. Elisei Rykov et al., Enoki: Efficient Multi-Level Hallucination Detection. v1 September 1; latest v2 September 4. Full PDF read: 22 pages, including appendices. PDF.

Enoki extracts text-anchored relational facts, checks them against supplied evidence and projects unsupported facts back to answer spans. With the shared explicit-verification pipeline, GPT-OSS-120B extraction scores 52.07 versus MinIE's 44.12 on MuSHROOM and 71.15 versus 64.06 on PsiloQA using Span Coverage F1. On RAGTruth, incremental Enoki scores 37.32, below 42.20 for a benchmark-finetuned comparator and 41.35 for Enoki without incremental prompting. Table 3, PDF p. 8.

Boundary: Span Coverage F1 rewards contained predictions and covered gold spans; it is not exact-boundary accuracy. Extraction omissions escape later verification. EnokiQA's annotations are largely automatic, so it is not independent human gold. The main text and Appendix G disagree on a quoted encoder latency; no speedup headline is adopted. Limitations and Appendices C, E, G.

BQAA TL;DR — inference: a review finding can carry the checked claim, evidence reference and proposed answer span together. Try this only as an optional aid within feedback correction, with human precision checks and an explicit unexamined remainder. Answer-span verification does not establish which agent step caused a failure; failure-localization work stays in reserve.
