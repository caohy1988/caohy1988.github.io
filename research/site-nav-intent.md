# Site navigation — intent

**Ask (Haiyuan, 2026-09-09):** make https://caohy1988.github.io better laid out: add a navigation bar and fold some tabs.

**Problem.** Every public page carried its own hand-copied dark strip of up to eleven flat links (Research, BQAA, CA in BigQuery, BQ ML / AI Ops, BQ Graph, BQ Lakehouse, BQ Search, Usage, RFC, Detailed RFC, EvalBench). The strips had drifted: the usage page listed four links, the RFC pages four different ones, the research boards eleven. On a phone the eleven links wrapped into three rows or overflowed. Nothing grouped the five BigQuery boards or the three build surfaces.

**Intent.** One shared site chrome with a short top level and two folders, generated into every page from a single definition so the strips cannot drift again, accessible from the keyboard, and collapsible on small screens. No new product pages, no change to article bodies, evidence claims, or the RFC word ceilings beyond the chrome itself.

**Non-goals.** Redesigning page mastheads; touching the archived Field Brief pages under `research/briefs/` (generated by the publisher bot with their own two-link strip); changing routes.
